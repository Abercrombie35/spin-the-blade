ABOUT

This page is for referencing various plugins for the minecraft server I host.

It is whitelisted for friends only (and because bots scrape for public servers), so just shoot me a message on discord to be added!

PLUGINS

These are largely quality of life plugins. They're meant to make life easier without altering the gameplay too much.
The lists below are non-exhaustive, but I tried including everything relevant to players. Enjoy!

Commands

  • Nether Portal Coords – /portal to get coordinates for linking.
  • Nickname – /nick set <text here> – change your name.
  • ItemLore - Supports color codes and MiniMessage.
    • /rename <text here> – Give an item a custom name.
    • /setlore <line number> – Add lore to your items.
    • /irr help – Displays commands and their usage.

    Tweaks

  • Fast Leaf Decay – Leaves will decay much faster.
  • Double Shulker Shells – Shulkers will drop 2 shells minimum.
  • Wandering Trader – Has mini blocks and hermit heads for sale!
  • Sandstone Dyeing – Red dye and sand makes red sandstone.
  • Back To Blocks – Turns stairs, and slabs back to blocks.
  • Universal Dyeing – Allows you to dye any dyeable block to another colour.
  • Weakened Bedrock – Throw a lingering potion of weakness to turn bedrock to cobblestone.
  • Silk Touch Amethyst – Budding Amethyst can be mined with a Silk Touch.
  • No Block Damage – Block damage caused by Creepers, Withers, Ender Dragon, and Fireballs is disabled.
  • Always Drop - Ender chests and glass blocks will always drop without the need for silk touch.
  • Cauldron Potions - texthere
  • Custom Nether Portals - Crying obsidian and portals with different dimensions can be lit.
  • More Mob Heads - More mobs have a chance to drop a head on death.
  • Player Head Drops - Kill your friends :D

    Craftables

  • Leather
    • – Place rotten flesh in furnace.
  • Black Dye
    • – Craft coal or charcoal.
  • Gilded Blackstone
    • – Craft 1 gold block, and 8 blackstone.
  • Cauldron Concrete
    • - While holding concrete powder, right-click a cauldron filled with water to turn into concrete.
  • Cauldron Mud
    • - While holding dirt, right-click a cauldron filled with water to turn into mud.

    Unpackables

  • Packed Ice – Turn 1 blue ice into 9 packed ice.
  • Ice – Turn 1 packed ice into 9 ice.
  • String – Turn 1 wool into 4 string.
  • Honeycomb – Turn 1 honeycomb block into 9 honeycomb.
